About this policy

This policy describes how Buenappi (“we”, “us”) handles information when you visit buenappi.com. It applies to this website and does not cover the privacy practices of any mobile app or third-party service; those will have their own notices where applicable.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your name, email address, and message. We use it to respond and manage our correspondence. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

Information collected automatically

Our website is designed as a simple static site. We do not intentionally ask for personal information through website forms or use advertising cookies on this sample site. The hosting provider may process basic technical data, such as IP address and request logs, to deliver and secure the website. Check your hosting configuration and update this section if you add analytics, forms, embedded content, or other services.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Information may be handled by service providers that help us operate the website or email, and may be disclosed where required by law. We retain correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to respond, keep appropriate records, or meet legal obligations.

Your choices

You can choose not to contact us. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain personal information. To ask a question or make a request, contact us at hello@buenappi.com.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the website or our practices change. The date above indicates when it was most recently revised. Questions about this policy? Get in touch.
